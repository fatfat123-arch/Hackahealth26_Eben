Original is left; neural result is right. The experimental runner limits clips
to ten seconds. Use a new output filename for each invocation.

Only TensorFlow imports are adapted in memory: tensorflow.compat.v1 and tf_slim
replace TF1 and tensorflow.contrib.slim. Original upstream files are unchanged.
Architecture, all 36 restored generator tensors, BGR normalization to [-1,1],
bilinear interpolation convention, and radius-1 guided filter (epsilon 0.005)
follow upstream inference. No random initialization is used for inference.

Benchmark frames remain 1280x720 at 30 fps, with no rescaling/cropping. Other sizes
are reflect-padded to multiples of eight, then unpadded after inference. The
existing cartoonize.py pipeline handles frame streaming, timestamp checks,
encoding, and synchronized audio. Its displayed OpenCV filter presets are
bypassed by this runner. The original OpenCV code and environment are unchanged.

This neural image model processes frames independently, without a temporal
consistency model. Repeated identical input was pixel-identical in the local
CPU test, but moving video can still flicker and fine gesture/face details can
change. Review playback and compare contact, fingers, and overlapping limbs.

whitebox_sample_5s.jpg is a native-size original/result comparison at second 5.
